# Historical results (local archive only)

The original figures and the previous three-image reproduction were preserved locally in `.local-archive/`, which is excluded from Git. The former `driving.png` input was identified by the project owner as originating from an NVIDIA dataset; its redistribution terms were not established for this publication. Historical figures may contain this image, so the entire historical figure collection is retained locally rather than uploaded.

The public results in `results/reproduced/` use `sample.jpg`, `coffee.jpg`, and the separately attributed CC0 `street.jpg`. Street results are newly computed with the same experiments 01–04 and must not be treated as the former driving-image results. The local archive also preserves historical `part_b` artifacts, whose generating script was absent and which are outside the scope of the repository's experiments and conclusions.

繁體中文：原始圖表、舊版重跑結果與原 NVIDIA 圖片保存在本機 `.local-archive/`，不加入 Git。公開版使用另有來源紀錄的 CC0 街景圖片，並重新執行相同的 01–04；新街景數值不等同於舊 driving 圖片的結果。
